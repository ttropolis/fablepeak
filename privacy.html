Privacy Policy

Effective 30 August 2026

What this policy covers

FablePeak is a social-media planning and publishing service. This policy explains what information we handle when you create a FablePeak account, create a workspace, or connect a third-party social account.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Workspace content such as brand names, drafts, schedules, media URLs, links and settings.
  • Connected-account information such as platform, account, Page or app-scoped user identifier, display name, avatar, granted permissions and connection status.
  • OAuth access and refresh credentials needed to publish or read the metrics you request. Provider credentials are kept server-side and application-encrypted before database storage.
  • Publishing results, supported analytics and limited technical diagnostics needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Aggregate SmartLink click counts for workspaces that have published a public SmartLinks page, as described below.
  • Workspace membership and invitation records, including the email addresses an owner has invited, as described below.

Public SmartLinks pages

A workspace can publish a SmartLinks page to a public web address. Publishing is opt-in, off by default and controlled by the workspace operator; nothing about a workspace is public until an operator turns it on.

  • When a page is published, these become world-readable to anyone with the address: the page address (slug), page title, bio text, avatar emoji, button colour, and each link's title, destination URL and order. Nothing else is published — not your workspace identifier, member list, email addresses, connected accounts, posts, inbox or metrics.
  • We count clicks on published links without identifying visitors. We do not store IP addresses, cookies, device identifiers, browser fingerprints or any persistent visitor identifier. Because of this, click counts are approximate by design and are labelled as such.
  • The only visitor-derived value we store is a coarse referring website hostname, such as example.com. Full referring addresses, paths and query strings are discarded before storage.
  • Individual click records are deleted after 90 days. Aggregate counts are kept only while the workspace exists and are deleted with it.
  • Unpublishing takes effect immediately: the page stops being readable and click collection stops. A page address that a workspace has previously used stays reserved to that workspace so it cannot be taken over by someone else.
  • Links on a published page point to third-party websites. Once a visitor follows a link, that destination site is governed by its own privacy policy, not ours.

Workspace members and invitations

A workspace can have more than one member. Members are either owners, who manage the workspace, or editors, who create and schedule content.

  • People in the same workspace can see each other's email addresses and roles. That is the only place your address is shown to another FablePeak user, and it happens only for workspaces you are a member of.
  • An owner invites someone by entering their email address. The invitation records that address, the offered role, who sent it and when it expires. It grants nothing on its own: no workspace content is readable until the invited person signs in with that same address, confirms it, and explicitly accepts.
  • Before an invitation is accepted, the invited person is shown only the workspace's display name and the offered role. They are not shown its members, content, connected accounts or identifiers.
  • Invitations expire after 14 days, and an owner can revoke a pending invitation at any time. FablePeak does not email invitations; the owner tells the invited person directly.
  • We keep a record of invitations, including the address they were sent to and whether they were accepted, declined, revoked or expired, for as long as the workspace exists. It is deleted with the workspace.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, sync your workspace, connect accounts you authorize, publish or schedule your content, show supported metrics, maintain connection health, prevent abuse and support you. We do not sell your personal information or use connected-account data for unrelated advertising.

When information is shared

We send information to a social platform only when needed for the feature you request—for example, sending a post to the account you selected. We also use infrastructure providers, including Supabase and web-hosting providers, to operate FablePeak. Those providers process data on our behalf. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

Workspace and connection data is retained while your account or workspace remains active. Disconnecting a social account removes its stored provider credentials from FablePeak. You may request deletion of your FablePeak account and associated data by following our data deletion instructions. Some records may be retained temporarily for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

Security

FablePeak uses tenant-scoped database access rules, server-only provider credentials, application-layer token encryption and short-lived OAuth state. No online service can guarantee absolute security; please contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

Your choices

You can choose which social accounts to connect, select the publishing account, disconnect an account, export workspace data, or request deletion. You can also revoke FablePeak directly from the connected platform’s security settings.

Children

FablePeak is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes and will revise the effective date above. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to ttropolis@gmail.com.
