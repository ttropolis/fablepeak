Disconnect one social account

  1. Sign in to FablePeak.
  2. Open the relevant workspace and choose Connections.
  3. Find the Facebook Page, Instagram profile or YouTube channel.
  4. Choose Disconnect and confirm.

This removes the stored provider credentials from FablePeak and stops future scheduled delivery to that account. You can also revoke FablePeak from the social platform’s own app/security settings.

Delete your FablePeak account and data

  1. Sign in to FablePeak.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Delete account, choose Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and enter your current password to confirm.

This removes your authentication record, memberships, account-associated provider credentials and workspaces owned only by you. Content in a shared workspace may remain for its other authorized members after your personal association is removed.

If you cannot sign in, send the request from your registered email address. Use the subject “Delete my FablePeak account.”

Request deletion by email

We will verify email requests and normally complete them within 30 days. We may retain limited records where required for security, legal obligations or dispute resolution.
